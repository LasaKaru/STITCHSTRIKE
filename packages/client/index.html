Co-op Defence

  • Three glowing Heartspools (A, B, C) power the house. Lose all three and the match is lost.
  • Between waves, spend the team's buttons on the embroidered pads: turrets shoot, pin walls block, tangle mats slow.
  • Unravel invaders to earn buttons. Brutes flatten buildables, Moths fly straight for the wool.
  • You have 200 stitches (plus thimble armour). A Yarn Basket refills every magazine. Stay out of trouble for 4 seconds and you knit back together.
  • Knocked down? A teammate holds E to re-stitch you. Everyone comes back when a wave is cleared.
  • Blockades make invaders walk the long way round: build a maze. Snips cut traps; Lance them first.
  • Mission: 10 waves and a boss. Skirmish: 5. Endless: forever.
  • Dino Stampede: raptors leap, trikes charge through traps, pteros swoop, and when Rex roars the whole herd rushes.
  • Capture the Yarn (PvP): grab the other team's yarn ball, run it home while yours is safe. First to 3.
  • Find golden thimbles, weapon parts and credit stashes hidden up high and in dark corners.
